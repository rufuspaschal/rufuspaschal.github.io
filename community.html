Community & Life

I’m a hardworking and active person who believes in continuous learning and staying fit. Outside of work, my life is about health, faith, and community — the things that keep me grounded and ready to serve.

Family & Home

Above all, I’m a devoted family man. After years of hard work, discipline, and perseverance in Canada, I recently celebrated one of life’s greatest joys — welcoming my family home to begin a new chapter together. My family is my foundation and my motivation: the reason I show up, work hard, and never give up.

My Interests

  • Fitness & body workouts — discipline for body and mind.
  • Lifelong learning — always eager to build new skills and knowledge.
  • Community engagement — networking and working with diverse people.
  • Hard work & dedication — reliable and committed in every role.
